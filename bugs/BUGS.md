## Entry format
```
- BUG-NNN | YYYY-MM-DD | OPEN|RESOLVED | <feature/module>
  - Symptom:    what was observed
  - Root cause: the underlying reason (not the symptom)
  - Fix:        what changed (file:line if useful)
```
